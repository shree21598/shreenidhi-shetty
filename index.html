Navy Federal Credit Union

AI/ML Engineer · Sep 2024 to present · Vienna, VA

  • Led technical discovery and production delivery of an AI platform for credit card disputes on Azure Functions, Blob Storage and Cosmos DB, with 3 enterprise integrations and 9 production alerts routed to ServiceNow.
  • Productionized the virtual assistant's RAG platform on Azure AI Search and Azure OpenAI, serving about 10,000 requests a month across 5 service domains.
  • Tuned RAG retrieval and generation with LangSmith traces, Application Insights and KQL, which raised throughput 1.8× and cut cost per request 22%. Added 6 Azure Monitor alerts on top.
  • Built MLOps workflows for 120+ models across fraud, marketing, savings and member engagement, with automated retraining, drift monitoring and champion/challenger evaluation.
  • Moved about 90 models into Databricks Unity Catalog and registered about 80 in MLflow for lineage and reproducible deployments.
  • Turned recurring deployment problems into reusable agent modules and taught them in workshops for 350+ engineers and product managers.
